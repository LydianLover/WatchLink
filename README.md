# WatchLink

An independent Android companion app for GloryFit Pro–family smartwatches (Actions ATS3085 watches such as the P131), built as a replacement for the official app.

- **Today** dashboard: steps ring, heart rate, blood oxygen, stress and more
- **Health** trends with day / week / month views and insights
- **Workouts** with GPS routes, pace and heart rate
- **Faces**: install, switch and delete watch faces (including built-ins), a full face editor, photo faces and always-on faces
- **Device**: notifications, calls, music, alarms, weather, contacts and every watch setting, all searchable

Everything stays on your phone. Not affiliated with UTE, GloryFit or the watch makers.

## Install

1. Download the latest `WatchLink-vX.Y.apk` from [Releases](../../releases/latest).
2. Open it on your phone and allow installing from your browser or file manager when Android asks.
3. Open WatchLink, go to **Device → Choose watch**, and pick your watch. Close the official app first: only one app can talk to the watch at a time.

## Updates

WatchLink checks this page for new releases by itself (or tap **Device → About & updates → Check for updates**) and installs them in a couple of taps. No app store needed.

## Watch not supported?

Connect it anyway. WatchLink will offer **Request support for this watch**: one tap reads what the watch can tell us (read-only, no health data or messages) and sends it to the developer so support can be added in an update.
